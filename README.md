# Valeria Leiva-Yamaguchi

### Statistician | Statistical Methodology | Survival & Multistate Models

I am a Statistician with a Bachelor's degree in Mathematics, an M.Sc. and a Ph.D. in Statistics.

My research focuses on the development and application of statistical methodology for complex longitudinal, survival, multistate and spatial data, with applications in biomedical research, epidemiology, cancer and environmental health.

## Research interests

- Survival and multistate models
- Joint models for longitudinal and time-to-event data
- Bayesian statistical methods
- Dynamic prediction and influence diagnostics
- Statistical methodology for biomedical and epidemiological research
- Spatial and geostatistical modelling
- Environmental exposure and exposome data

## Research experience

I have conducted research at the MRC Biostatistics Unit, University of Cambridge, working on statistical methodology for complex longitudinal and survival data.

My current research includes methodological developments in multistate models and their applications to cancer research, as well as emerging work in spatial statistics, geostatistical modelling and environmental exposure.
